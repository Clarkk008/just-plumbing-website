Why Material Selection Matters in Colorado

Front Range homes experience sub-zero freeze cycles, hard mineral content, and high municipal pressure spikes. Choosing the wrong piping material or using low-bid crimp fittings leads to premature failures, reduced shower pressure, and burst lines during winter freezes.
