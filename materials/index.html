Our High-Performance Piping Systems

Colorado's high elevation, freezing winter temperatures, and municipal water treatment additives demand piping materials that withstand intense hydraulic and thermal stress. Explore our primary piping standards below:

Uponor PEX-A Expansion Piping

Our gold standard for whole-home residential repiping. Cross-linked polyethylene with thermal shape-memory expands safely if water freezes, while full-port ProPEX fittings deliver maximum water volume.

  • Fitting Type: Full-port expansion rings (no crimp rings)
  • Freeze Tolerance: Expands up to 300% without splitting
  • Warranty: 25-year manufacturer + 5-year workmanship

Why We Refuse to Install Crimp-Style PEX-B

Many low-bid repiping contractors cut corners by using PEX-B tubing secured with copper crimp rings. To make a crimp connection, an undersized fitting must be inserted into the pipe, creating a permanent 20% to 30% constriction at every single tee, elbow, and turn.

When multiple showers or fixtures run simultaneously, homes plumbed with PEX-B experience severe pressure drops. Just Plumbing exclusively installs Uponor PEX-A using full-port expansion rings that expand the pipe temporarily, allowing fittings with the exact same inner diameter as the pipe itself.

The result is 100% full water volume, whisper-quiet operation, and a plumbing system engineered to last for generations.

Frequently Asked Questions — Piping Materials

Why does Just Plumbing exclusively recommend Uponor PEX-A over PEX-B?

Uponor PEX-A utilizes the Engel-method cross-linking process, giving the pipe thermal shape memory. It expands with freezing water to prevent bursting, and connects with full-port expansion fittings that maintain maximum water volume. Cheaper PEX-B requires insert crimp fittings that restrict water flow by 20% to 30% at every joint.

When is Type L Copper preferred over PEX-A?

Type L rigid copper is the ideal specification for exposed mechanical rooms, boiler loops, commercial risers, and historic homes where local architectural review boards or strict municipal building codes mandate metallic water lines.

What warranties back your repiping materials?

Uponor PEX-A systems installed by Just Plumbing carry a 25-year manufacturer material warranty paired with our 5-year Master Plumber workmanship warranty.
