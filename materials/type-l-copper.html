Type L Rigid Copper Piping Systems

Thick-walled, commercial-grade copper plumbing installed with precision Master Plumber solder work.

For homeowners and commercial properties requiring rigid metal plumbing, we install premium Type L copper for maximum strength, UV resistance, and code compliance.

Get Your Copper Repipe Quote

Why Choose Type L Copper

Heavy-Duty Wall Thickness

We exclusively install Type L copper (blue stamp), which is 50% thicker than builder-grade Type M (red stamp) for maximum pressure tolerance.

Master Plumber Soldering

Clean, deburred pipe ends and precision lead-free soldering prevent interior turbulence and turbulent wear at joints.

100% Recyclable & Fireproof

Copper is an all-natural, biostatic material that will not melt or release toxic fumes in the event of a structural fire.

When is Copper Preferred in Colorado?

1. Water Heater & Boiler Transitions

Colorado plumbing codes require metallic piping within the first 18 inches of gas-fired water heaters and boiler systems to safely handle high thermal exhaust temperatures and radiant heat.

2. Historic Restorations & Galvanized Upgrades

When removing corroded galvanized steel piping in historic Denver neighborhoods like Park Hill or Wash Park, Type L rigid copper preserves architectural authenticity while delivering pristine drinking water quality.

3. Outdoor & UV-Exposed Piping

While Uponor PEX-A is our premier choice for concealed residential runs, polymer tubing degrades under direct sunlight. For exterior hose bibbs, above-ground commercial risers, and rooftop lines, Type L rigid copper provides lifelong UV and physical impact resistance.
