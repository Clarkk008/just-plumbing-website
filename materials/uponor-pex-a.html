Uponor PEX Type A Expansion Piping

The industry's gold standard in residential repiping. Unmatched flexibility, full water flow, and zero crimp rings.

We install Uponor ProPEX Type A expansion systems backed by a 25-year manufacturer warranty on materials and our 5-year workmanship warranty.

Why Master Plumbers Choose Uponor PEX-A

Full-Port Water Flow

Unlike PEX-B crimp rings that choke inner diameter by up to 30%, ProPEX expansion fittings maintain full internal pipe diameter for high water pressure.

Freeze-Burst Resistance

Thermal shape memory allows PEX-A to expand during deep Colorado freezes and contract back to shape once thawed without splitting.

Chemical Immunity

Completely immune to pitting corrosion, scale build-up, and municipal chlorine breakdown that destroys copper and polybutylene.

The ProPEX Cold-Expansion Advantage

Shape Memory Engineering & Polybutylene Remediation

Manufactured using the Engel cross-linking method, Uponor PEX-A has true thermal memory. The pipe and expansion ring are briefly expanded with a mechanical tool, the fitting is inserted, and the pipe naturally shrinks down tightly around the fitting, creating a permanent watertight seal without glue, torches, or mechanical crimps.

Engineered with shape memory and thermal elasticity, Uponor PEX-A expansion fittings eliminate the rigid pinch points responsible for sudden plumbing failures. When replacing brittle polybutylene systems across older Denver and Douglas County communities, PEX-A provides the flexibility needed to navigate framing with minimal wall incisions while completely resisting chlorine-induced micro-fractures. See our real-world installation in the Highlands Ranch Polybutylene Case Study.

Quieter Plumbing & No Water Hammer

PEX-A dampens the sound of rushing water and absorbs pressure surges from quick-closing appliance valves (dishwashers and washing machines), eliminating banging pipes and water hammer throughout your home.

Replacing Deteriorated Metal Lines

Upgrading to PEX-A solves chronic problems caused by aging systems, including rusty flow from galvanized steel lines and wall damage from recurring copper pinhole leaks. Where metallic transitions are required by municipal code, we pair PEX-A with commercial-grade Type L rigid copper.
