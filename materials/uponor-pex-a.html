Why Master Plumbers Choose Uponor PEX-A

Full-Port Water Flow

Unlike PEX-B crimp rings that choke inner diameter by up to 30%, ProPEX expansion fittings maintain full internal pipe diameter for high water pressure.

Freeze-Burst Resistance

Thermal shape memory allows PEX-A to expand during deep Colorado freezes and contract back to shape once thawed without splitting.

Chemical Immunity

Completely immune to pitting corrosion, scale build-up, and municipal chlorine breakdown that destroys copper and polybutylene.

The ProPEX Cold-Expansion Advantage

Shape Memory Engineering

Manufactured using the Engel cross-linking method, Uponor PEX-A has true thermal memory. The pipe and expansion ring are briefly expanded with a mechanical tool, the fitting is inserted, and the pipe naturally shrinks down tightly around the fitting, creating a permanent watertight seal without glue, torches, or mechanical crimps.

Quieter Plumbing & No Water Hammer

PEX-A dampens the sound of rushing water and absorbs pressure surges from quick-closing appliance valves (dishwashers and washing machines), eliminating banging pipes and water hammer.
