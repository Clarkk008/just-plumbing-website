Why Uponor PEX-A Is Our Undisputed Flagship Piping Material

When repiping a residential property in the Colorado Front Range, the choice of piping material determines whether the installation will last for decades or develop leaks within a few short years. At Just Plumbing, Inc., we refuse to install builder-grade PEX-B with mechanical crimp rings. Instead, we exclusively install Uponor PEX-A (AquaPEX) utilizing ASTM F1960 ProPEX cold-expansion fittings.

The Engineering Advantages of Uponor PEX-A

1. The Engel Method & Molecular Thermal Shape Memory

PEX-A is manufactured using the hot-extrusion Engel method, where high-density polyethylene is cross-linked during the molten state above its crystalline melting point. This creates uniform, 3-dimensional molecular bonds achieving over 85% cross-linking density. The practical benefit is extraordinary flexibility and "thermal shape memory"—if the pipe is accidentally kinked during an installation, it can be gently heated with a hot-air gun to return 100% to its original factory form and structural integrity, without cutting or compromising the line.

2. ProPEX Full-Port Expansion (ASTM F1960) vs. Crimp Rings

Standard PEX-B installations require crimping an undersized fitting into the pipe with a copper or stainless steel ring, creating an immediate 20% to 30% reduction in internal diameter at every elbow and tee. Uponor ProPEX connections work in reverse: an expansion tool briefly enlarges the pipe and an outer PEX ring. The fitting is inserted, and the pipe shrinks back around the fitting with thousands of pounds of radial force. Because the fitting has the same inner diameter as the pipe, water flows with zero restriction, maintaining maximum shower volume and fixture pressure.

3. Sub-Zero Freeze-Burst Resilience

Colorado winters frequently bring sustained sub-zero temperatures that freeze unconditioned crawlspaces, cantilevers, and exterior wall plumbing. Rigid copper pipe walls and glued CPVC plastic fracture immediately when ice expands by 9% in volume. Uponor PEX-A expands up to three times its standard diameter without rupturing. As the thaw occurs, the molecular memory pulls the tubing back to its original dimensions, providing unmatched freeze protection for foothills and metro homes alike.

4. 100% Immunity to Chloramines, Scale & Pinhole Corrosion

Front Range water utilities use chemical disinfectants (chlorine and chloramines) that cause pitting corrosion in copper and rapid brittle degradation in legacy polybutylene. Uponor PEX-A is completely chemically inert. It will never rust, pit, scale, or leach metal into drinking water, ensuring pristine taste and full flow rates for the lifetime of your home.

Manufacturer Warranty & Certification Standards

Uponor PEX-A is certified to ASTM F876, ASTM F877, ASTM F1960, and NSF/ANSI Standards 14 and 61 for potable water distribution, meeting or exceeding all International Plumbing Code (IPC) and Colorado state mechanical mandates.

Frequently Asked Questions — Uponor PEX-A

What makes Uponor PEX-A superior to standard PEX-B?

Uponor PEX-A is manufactured using the Engel method, which achieves over 85% cross-linking across the polymer chains during extrusion. This gives the pipe uniform flexibility and thermal shape memory. Unlike PEX-B, which uses crimp rings that constrict flow by 20% to 30%, Uponor uses full-port ProPEX expansion fittings that maintain maximum diameter and unrestricted water pressure.

Can Uponor PEX-A freeze without bursting in Colorado winters?

Yes. Uponor PEX-A can expand up to 300% of its standard diameter when water freezes into ice. Because it possesses thermal shape memory, the pipe simply stretches to absorb the expanding ice volume and naturally contracts back to factory dimensions when thawed, preventing the burst seams common in copper and CPVC.

How does the Uponor ProPEX expansion connection work?

A specialized expansion tool temporarily enlarges the PEX-A pipe end and an engineered PEX ring. A full-flow brass or engineered polymer fitting is inserted while the pipe is expanded. Within seconds, the material's molecular memory forces it to contract tightly around the fitting, creating a permanent, watertight joint that actually gets stronger over time.

Every whole-home Uponor PEX-A system installed by Just Plumbing is backed by Uponor's 25-year transferable manufacturer material warranty and our 5-year Master Plumber workmanship warranty.
