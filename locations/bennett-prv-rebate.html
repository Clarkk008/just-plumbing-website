Why Bennett Homes Need PRVs and Plumbing Upgrades

Over the past decade, the Town of Bennett constructed a half-million-gallon elevated water storage tank to stabilize and increase town water pressure. While this is great for the town's expanding infrastructure, it poses a severe threat to older residential plumbing systems. Homes without a properly calibrated Pressure Reducing Valve (PRV) are subjected to extreme pressure spikes.

High water pressure acts like a wrecking ball on aging plumbing. If your home in Bennett was built with polybutylene plastic or galvanized steel, the aggressive municipal pressure will rapidly accelerate pinhole leaks, burst fittings, and blow out the seals on your water heater.

When searching for a trusted plumber in Bennett, CO, Just Plumbing, Inc. brings over 30 years of Master Plumber expertise. We don't just install your PRV so you can claim your Town of Bennett rebate—we provide comprehensive whole-home repiping using premium Uponor PEX-A to completely bulletproof your home's water system against future pressure surges and leaks.

How the $550 Bennett PRV Rebate Works

To qualify for the rebate, your home must be connected to the Town of Bennett water distribution system. The PRV must be installed by a licensed professional with a proper permit (the town waives the permit fee).

Just Plumbing makes it easy: We handle the permitting, install the compliant valve, and hand you the official receipt with our Master Plumber License (#MP.00600120). You simply email that receipt to prv@bennett.co.us within 60 days to get your cash back!

Frequently Asked Questions — Bennett Plumbing

What happens if I don't install a PRV in my Bennett home?

Without a Pressure Reducing Valve, your home's plumbing is subjected to full municipal pressure, which can easily exceed 80 psi. This constant stress damages washing machine hoses, blows out water heater elements, and can cause catastrophic pipe bursts inside your walls.

Will a PRV fix my currently leaking pipes?

No. While a PRV regulates pressure to prevent future damage, if your old galvanized or polybutylene pipes have already degraded and are leaking, they must be completely extracted and replaced via a whole-home repipe.

How do I submit for the Bennett PRV rebate?

Once Just Plumbing completes the code-compliant installation, you have 60 days to fill out the application on the Town of Bennett's website and email it, along with our itemized master plumber receipt, to prv@bennett.co.us.
