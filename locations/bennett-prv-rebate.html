Why Do You Need a PRV in Bennett?

Over the past decade, the Town of Bennett constructed a half-million-gallon elevated water storage tank to stabilize and increase water pressure. While this is great for the town's infrastructure, homes without a Pressure Reducing Valve (PRV) may experience unnecessary stress on their internal plumbing.

  • Protect Your Pipes: High water pressure causes undue wear and tear, leading to leaks, blown fittings, or pipe fatigue.
  • Save Appliances: Your water heater, washing machine, and dishwasher aren't built to handle pressure exceeding 80 psi.
  • Meet Modern Codes: Current plumbing codes dictate that a PRV must be installed on homes where municipal pressure is high.

Rebate Eligibility & How it Works

To qualify for the **$550 rebate**, your home must be connected to the Town of Bennett water distribution system and typically be located in neighborhoods like Centennial, Brothers Four, Cordella, or Old Town.

Local Water Chemistry & Pressure Challenges

High municipal supply pressures (often exceeding 80–100 PSI) and localized mineral chemistry frequently cause copper pinhole leaks and slab leaks in this community. Patching individual pinholes only redirects pressure to adjacent weak spots in degraded copper walls.

Our Master Plumber-led team performs clean whole-home conversions using corrosion-immune Uponor PEX-A expansion lines paired with heavy-duty Pressure Reducing Valves (PRVs) to protect your fixtures and water heaters.
