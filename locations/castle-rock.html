Frequently Asked Questions — Castle Rock Plumbing

How does hard water affect water heaters in Castle Rock, CO?

Castle Rock water contains high concentrations of calcium and magnesium minerals. Over time, these minerals settle at the bottom of traditional water heater tanks, forming a thick scale layer that causes element burnout, loud popping sounds, and premature tank failure. Regular sediment flushing or converting to a high-efficiency tankless system protects your investment.

Why is PEX Type A ideal for Castle Rock homes?

PEX Type A expansion piping is impervious to hard water mineral corrosion and scale pitting that affects metal lines. Additionally, PEX expands up to 300% during winter freezes, making it an ideal choice for whole-home repipes during severe Douglas County cold snaps.
