Solving Aurora's Top Plumbing Challenges

1. Water Heater Failure & Sediment Scaling

Because Aurora water is hard, mineral sediment bakes onto the bottom of traditional gas and electric storage tanks, creating a thick crust. Water gets trapped underneath this crust, overheats, and causes loud popping or rumbling noises before the tank metal fatigues and splits open. We perform routine sediment flushes, component repairs, and precision installations of tankless water heaters built to handle high-mineral inflow.

2. Polybutylene Pipe Bursts in 80s & 90s Subdivisions

Thousands of homes built during Aurora's building boom in the 1980s and 1990s were plumbed with flexible gray Polybutylene piping. Combined with chemical municipal water treatments, these lines deteriorate internally, leading to sudden, catastrophic wall leaks. If your home in Mission Viejo or Meadow Hills still has polybutylene, our team can systematically repipe your entire home in just 1 to 2 days—including hiring the drywall patcher to restore your walls.

Common Plumbing System Failures in This Area

Subdivisions built between 1995 and 2007 in this corridor frequently contain defective Kitec composite piping and brass fittings. Kitec fittings suffer from dezincification—forming a white powdery corrosion ("meringue") that weakens connections until fittings shear off under normal pressure.

We eliminate these systemic failures by replacing the entire piping footprint with certified Uponor PEX Type A expansion piping. Compare the differences between fitting systems in our PEX-A vs. PEX-B guide.

Frequently Asked Questions — Aurora Plumbing

Why do water heaters fail so frequently in Aurora, CO?

Aurora's water supply contains elevated levels of dissolved minerals like calcium and magnesium. When heated inside a standard storage tank, these minerals precipitate into a thick scale layer at the bottom. This scale traps heat, forcing the tank bottom to overheat, crack, and fail prematurely unless routinely descaled or flushed.

Should I upgrade to a tankless water heater in my Aurora home?

Yes, high-efficiency tankless water heaters are ideal for Aurora homes because they heat water on demand without allowing mineral sediment to settle in a standing reservoir. Master Plumber Bill Clark ensures correct BTU sizing to handle Aurora's cold winter groundwater temperatures.
