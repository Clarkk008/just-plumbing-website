Polybutylene & Plumbing Specialists in Highlands Ranch

Highlands Ranch is one of Douglas County's premier master-planned communities. However, because thousands of homes in Northridge, Southridge, Eastridge, and Westridge were constructed during the building boom of the 1980s and 1990s, homeowners face a widespread hidden risk: defective gray Polybutylene piping.

Over decades of exposure to municipal water treatments, polybutylene pipes degrade internally, become brittle, and fracture at plastic acetal fittings—leading to sudden catastrophic leaks behind sheetrock. Many insurance companies now refuse coverage or drop policies on Highlands Ranch homes with active polybutylene lines.

At Just Plumbing, Inc., Master Plumber Bill Clark brings 30+ years of local experience replacing polybutylene with freeze-resistant, scale-proof PEX Type A expansion piping. We complete full-home repipes in 1 to 3 days with minimal wall disruption—and we manage the professional drywall restoration for you!

Prevalent Plumbing Risks in This Area

Homes built throughout this area during the 1980s and 1990s housing boom frequently contain failing polybutylene (Quest) piping. Municipal water disinfectants degrade these gray plastic pipes from the inside out, creating sudden burst risks and insurance cancellation notices.

We permanently resolve these risks in 1–2 days by upgrading your domestic water system to full-flow Uponor PEX-A expansion piping. Learn why Master Plumbers choose PEX-A over standard PEX-B for cold-weather freeze durability.

Frequently Asked Questions — Highlands Ranch Plumbing

How do I check if my Highlands Ranch home has polybutylene pipes?

Look at exposed plumbing near your main water shutoff valve, basement ceiling joists, or under bathroom sinks. Polybutylene is a flexible gray plastic pipe usually stamped with "PB2110". If you see gray pipes, contact us via our contact page for a free inspection.

How long does a whole-home repipe take in Highlands Ranch?

Most Highlands Ranch repiping jobs take between 1 to 3 days. Water service is safely restored at the end of each workday so your household remains fully operational throughout the process.

Who manages plumbing permits in Douglas County?

Just Plumbing, Inc. operates under Colorado Plumbing Contractor Registration #PC.0003531 and Master Plumber License #MP.00600120. We pull all required permits through Douglas County Building Services and manage required city sign-offs.
