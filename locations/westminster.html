Repiping & Master Plumbing Specialists in Westminster

Positioned along the US-36 corridor between Denver and Boulder, Westminster is a large, diverse city spanning both Adams and Jefferson counties. Because Westminster features neighborhoods built across several distinct eras, property owners encounter different plumbing issues depending on their location.

Homes in South Westminster built in the mid-20th century frequently contain corroded galvanized steel lines that choke water flow and pollute tap water. Meanwhile, master-planned developments near Standley Lake, Countryside, and Ranch Reserve built during the 1980s and 1990s often contain defective gray polybutylene pipes, which break down from chemical water treatments and trigger sudden indoor wall floods.

Common Plumbing System Failures in This Area

Subdivisions built between 1995 and 2007 in this corridor frequently contain defective Kitec composite piping and brass fittings. Kitec fittings suffer from dezincification—forming a white powdery corrosion ("meringue") that weakens connections until fittings shear off under normal pressure.

We eliminate these systemic failures by replacing the entire piping footprint with certified Uponor PEX Type A expansion piping. Compare the differences between fitting systems in our PEX-A vs. PEX-B guide.

Frequently Asked Questions — Westminster Plumbing

How do I check if my Westminster home has polybutylene pipes?

Check exposed piping near your main water shutoff valve, basement ceiling joists, or under bathroom sinks. Polybutylene is a flexible gray plastic pipe usually stamped with "PB2110". If you spot gray lines, contact us via our contact page to schedule an inspection.

How long does a whole-home repipe take in Westminster?

Most Westminster repiping projects take between 1 to 3 days depending on home size. Main water service is safely restored at the end of each workday so your household remains fully operational throughout the process.

Who manages plumbing permits in the City of Westminster?

Just Plumbing, Inc. operates under Colorado Plumbing Contractor Registration #PC.0003531 and Master Plumber License #MP.00600120. We pull all required permits directly through the City of Westminster Building Division and handle all required municipal sign-offs.
