Historic & Established Home Plumbing Risks

Homes built prior to the 1960s in this area frequently suffer from internal corrosion and choked water pressure caused by original galvanized steel piping. As zinc coatings wear away, internal rust narrows pipe diameters to the width of a straw, causing discolored water and poor shower volume.

We surgically replace old galvanized systems without destroying historic plaster or trim, installing modern Uponor PEX-A or commercial-grade Type L rigid copper lines. Learn more about fitting dynamics in our PEX-A vs. PEX-B Guide.

Frequently Asked Questions — Denver Plumbing

Why do historic homes in Denver neighborhoods like Wash Park or Highlands need repiping?

Denver homes constructed prior to 1960 were largely plumbed with galvanized steel pipes. Over decades, galvanized steel rusts internally, severely restricting water pressure and shedding iron particles into tap water. Just Plumbing specializes in surgical PEX Type A repiping that modernizes plumbing without damaging historic lath-and-plaster or brick character.

How does Denver's high altitude affect water heater installations?

At 5,280 feet, thinner air reduces oxygen availability for gas combustion. Gas water heaters installed in Denver require specific high-altitude burner derating and proper venting calibration to ensure safe carbon monoxide exhaust and prevent heat exchanger burnout. Just Plumbing handles all altitude-specific configurations.

Does Just Plumbing handle permits through the City and County of Denver?

Yes. Just Plumbing, Inc. operates under Colorado Plumbing Contractor Registration #PC.0003531 and Master Plumber License #MP.00600120. We pull all necessary permits directly through the City and County of Denver Community Planning & Development Department and manage all required inspections.
