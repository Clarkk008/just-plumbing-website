Repiping & Master Plumbing Specialists in Thornton

Situated along the I-25 North corridor in Adams County, Thornton is one of the North Metro area's largest and most vibrant communities. However, because Thornton spans decades of rapid residential development, homeowners encounter vastly different plumbing challenges depending on their neighborhood's age.

Older homes in Original Thornton built during the 1950s and 1960s frequently contain corroded galvanized steel lines that choke off water pressure and leach iron rust into taps. Meanwhile, homes in Thorncreek, Signal Creek, Eastlake, and Heritage Todd Creek built during the 1980s and 1990s often contain defective gray polybutylene piping, which degrades internally and causes sudden wall bursts.

At Just Plumbing, Inc., Master Plumber Bill Clark brings 30+ years of local experience replacing galvanized and polybutylene systems with freeze-resistant, scale-proof PEX Type A expansion piping. We complete full-home repipes in 1 to 3 days with minimal wall disruption—and we manage the professional drywall restoration for you!

Frequently Asked Questions — Thornton Plumbing

How do I know if my Thornton home needs a full repipe?

Key warning signs include a significant drop in water pressure when multiple fixtures run, discolored or rusty water coming from hot water taps, recurring pinhole leaks in sheetrock, or flexible gray plastic pipes stamped "PB2110". Contact us via our contact page to schedule a consultation.

How long does a whole-home repipe take in Thornton?

Most Thornton repiping projects take between 1 to 3 days depending on home size. Main water service is safely restored at the end of every work day so your home remains completely livable throughout the project.

Who manages plumbing permits in the City of Thornton?

Just Plumbing, Inc. operates under Colorado Plumbing Contractor Registration #PC.0003531 and Master Plumber License #MP.00600120. We pull all necessary permits through the City of Thornton City Development Department and handle all required municipal sign-offs.
