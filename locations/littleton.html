Historic Home & Residential Plumbing Experts in Littleton, Colorado

Littleton offers a rich history and distinct character, featuring everything from late 19th-century homes surrounding Historic Downtown Main Street to mid-century ranches and established developments in neighborhoods like Broadridge, Slatterly Park, and Normandy. However, these charming homes frequently carry concealed piping liabilities inside plaster walls and crawlspaces: aging, heavily corroded galvanized iron pipes or brittle polybutylene plumbing lines.

If you live in one of Littleton's established neighborhoods, your home's original water distribution system may be near the end of its useful lifespan. When looking for a proven plumber in Littleton, CO, Just Plumbing, Inc. brings over 30 years of Master Plumber experience to work safely inside older and custom homes across the Greater Denver Metro area. We specialize in precision, minimally invasive whole-home repiping that replaces failing water lines with durable, freeze-resistant Uponor PEX-A (backed by a 25-year material warranty and 5-year workmanship warranty) or rigid Type L Copper—restoring strong water pressure and safeguarding your property's value. Our Top Littleton Plumbing Services

  • Internal Whole-Home Repiping: Replacing deteriorated galvanized or polybutylene pipes with modern PEX Type A or copper, eliminating rusty water and hidden leak risks.
  • Partial Repipes & Remodels: Targeted line upgrades for exposed basement supply lines, crawlspaces, or active kitchen and bathroom renovations.
  • Water Heater Systems: Code-compliant installations and quick repairs for traditional gas/electric storage tank heaters and high-efficiency tankless units.
  • Sump Pumps & Battery Backups: High-performance sump pump installs and battery backups to protect Littleton basements from South Platte River basin water tables and spring storms.

Prevalent Plumbing Risks in This Area

Homes built throughout this area during the 1980s and 1990s housing boom frequently contain failing polybutylene (Quest) piping. Municipal water disinfectants degrade these gray plastic pipes from the inside out, creating sudden burst risks and insurance cancellation notices.

We permanently resolve these risks in 1–2 days by upgrading your domestic water system to full-flow Uponor PEX-A expansion piping. Learn why Master Plumbers choose PEX-A over standard PEX-B for cold-weather freeze durability.

Frequently Asked Questions — Littleton Plumbing

How do I know if my Littleton home needs new pipes?

Common indicators in older Littleton homes include sudden pressure drops when running multiple fixtures, brownish water when turning on hot taps, rust flakes in faucet aerators, or mystery damp spots on drywall. Contact us via our contact page to schedule a detailed plumbing inspection.

Do you pull permits for plumbing projects in Littleton?

Yes. Just Plumbing, Inc. is a licensed Colorado Plumbing Contractor (#PC.0003531) led by Master Plumber Bill Clark (#MP.00600120). We handle all necessary permit filings through the City of Littleton Building Division or local county jurisdictions and coordinate all required city inspections.

How long does a whole-house repipe take in a Littleton home?

Most residential repiping projects in Littleton take 1 to 3 days, depending on layout, home size, and access points. We work cleanly and ensure running water service is re-established at the end of every day.
