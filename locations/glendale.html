Multi-Family & Commercial Plumbing Solutions Across Glendale

Glendale is a unique municipal enclave along Cherry Creek, bordered entirely by Denver. Known for high-density residential corridors near Infinity Park, Cherry Creek Trail, and Colorado Boulevard, zip code 80246 contains one of the largest concentrations of multi-family apartments, condominium communities, and commercial residential properties in the metro area. Constructed largely throughout the 1960s, 1970s, and 1980s, these properties feature shared vertical supply risers and central boiler loops that are failing due to age and heavy water demand.

When a shared vertical copper riser suffers pinhole erosion or a 1980s polybutylene fitting shears off, water cascades through multiple stacked units, leading to tenant displacement and insurance premium spikes. See how we manage complex multi-unit piping in our HOA Condo Repipe Case Study.

When property managers, HOA boards, and homeowners need an experienced plumber in Glendale, CO, Just Plumbing, Inc. brings over three decades of Master Plumber field leadership. We specialize in phased, tenant-safe multi-family repiping using scale- and erosion-immune Uponor PEX-A (backed by a 25-year material warranty and 5-year workmanship warranty) and commercial-grade Type L Copper, keeping water outages brief and localized.

Phased Riser Retrofits Without Tenant Displacement

Shutting down water to an entire multi-family building in Glendale creates severe resident friction and management liabilities.

Our repiping protocols isolate plumbing on a stack-by-stack basis. We map vertical risers, coordinate clear notification schedules, and pull flexible Uponor PEX-A expansion runs through existing mechanical chases. Water service is fully restored every afternoon, guaranteeing compliance with local rental housing codes.

Frequently Asked Questions — Glendale Plumbing

Why do multi-family buildings and condos in Glendale frequently need repiping?

Glendale features one of the highest densities of multi-family apartments and condominiums in the Denver Metro area, mostly constructed in the 1960s, 70s, and 80s. Decades of continuous water use, recirculating loop velocity erosion, and thin copper walls cause recurring pinhole leaks along shared risers, risking water damage across multiple stacked units.

How does Just Plumbing handle repiping in occupied Glendale rental communities?

We execute phased riser retrofits designed to avoid tenant displacement. Water service is isolated by vertical stack rather than shutting down the entire complex, and domestic water is restored to all units at the end of each working day.

Are plumbing permits required for repiping and commercial water heaters in Glendale, CO?

Yes. The City of Glendale Building Division requires permits and inspections for all multi-family riser repiping and commercial water heater installations.
