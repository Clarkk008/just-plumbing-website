Frequently Asked Questions — About Our Company

What qualifications and state licenses does Just Plumbing, Inc. hold?

Just Plumbing, Inc. operates under Colorado Plumbing Contractor Registration #PC.0003531 and Master Plumber License #MP.00600120. We are fully insured, bonded, and registered with the Colorado Department of Regulatory Agencies (DORA).
