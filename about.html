Our Technical Standards & Material Philosophy

We never cut corners with cheap builder-grade crimp fittings or inferior tubing. Every installation adheres strictly to Colorado plumbing code:

Uponor PEX-A Expansion Piping

We install ASTM F1960 cold-expansion Uponor AquaPEX tubing. Thermal shape memory makes it highly resistant to freeze bursts, scaling, and aggressive water chloramines. Read our comparison in the PEX-A vs. PEX-B Guide.

Type L Heavy-Wall Copper

For homeowners who prefer metal or historic properties requiring non-combustible materials, we install heavy-wall Type L Copper with lead-free solder and ProPress fittings. Learn more in our Type L Copper Specification.

Defective Pipe Remediation

We safely extract hazardous polybutylene, corroded galvanized steel, and recalled Kitec pipes, issuing official Master Plumber completion certificates for insurance policies and title companies.
