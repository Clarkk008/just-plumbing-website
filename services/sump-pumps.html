Why Sump Pumps Fail in Denver

Denver Metro homeowners face a unique combination of rapid spring snowmelts and severe summer monsoons. When power lines go down during a storm, a standard electric pump stops working exactly when you need it most. At Just Plumbing, Inc., we build resilient, redundant systems to prevent this.

If an aging plumbing system or corroded metal lines are also placing your basement at risk, explore our whole-home repiping or partial repiping services using freeze-resistant Uponor PEX-A (backed by a 25-year material warranty and 5-year workmanship warranty).

Signs Your Pump is Failing:

  • Constant Cycling: The pump turns on and off every few seconds (usually a stuck float switch or bad check valve).
  • Loud Grinding Noises: Indicates a jammed impeller or failing motor bearings.
  • Old Age: Pumps older than 7–10 years are statistically likely to fail during the next major storm.
  • Corrosion: Visible rust on the pump casing or deteriorating galvanized lines nearby.

Our Premium Sump Pump Solutions

From rapid component repairs to intelligent dual-battery arrays, we protect your foundation.

Cast-Iron Submersible Installations

We discard cheap plastic pedestal pumps in favor of heavy-duty, cast-iron submersible pumps. These sit at the bottom of the pit, using the groundwater to cool their motors, ensuring a longer, quieter lifespan.

Dual-Battery Backup Systems

The ultimate insurance policy. We install an intelligent secondary pump paired with deep-cycle marine batteries. If the power grid fails, or your primary pump burns out, the backup takes over automatically and sounds an alarm.

Check Valve Replacements

If your pump cycles continuously, water is likely draining back into the pit after being pumped out. We install quiet, positive-seal check valves to eliminate backflow and stop the annoying "clunk" sound.

Freeze-Proof Discharge Lines

Colorado winter cold snaps can freeze shallow discharge lines solid. We grade external pipes properly and install specialized freeze-relief air gaps so your pump never burns out trying to push water against a wall of ice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a basement sump pump usually last in Colorado?

Most high-quality submersible sump pumps last between 7 to 10 years depending on usage frequency, water table levels, and mechanical wear. Annual inspection by a licensed plumber ensures reliable operation before heavy spring rain.

Do I really need a battery backup sump pump system in Denver?

Yes. Severe Colorado summer storms and heavy spring snowmelts often cause power outages right when groundwater influx is at its peak. A dual battery backup system ensures your pump keeps running even during extended electrical blackouts, saving thousands in carpet and drywall damage.

How often should I test my basement sump pump?

You should test your sump pump at least twice a year. Simply pour a bucket of water directly into the basin; the float switch should rise, trigger the motor smoothly, pump the water out, and shut off automatically.
