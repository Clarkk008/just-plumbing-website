Specialized Infrastructure Plumbing

We are not general handymen or high-pressure franchise drain clearers. We focus on complex plumbing infrastructure: replacing failed piping materials, restoring high-volume water pressure, and engineering dependable mechanical systems.

Whole-Home Repiping

Our signature service. Turnkey replacement of failing polybutylene, rusted galvanized steel, recalled Kitec, and pitting copper. Completed in 1–2 days using Uponor PEX-A expansion tubing or Type L Copper, with water service restored every evening.

  • Timeline: 1 to 2 days average turnaround
  • Materials: Uponor PEX-A (25-yr warranty) or Type L Copper
  • Impact: Minimally invasive with daily water restoration

Partial Repiping & Slab Bypasses

Targeted line replacements and overhead reroutes. When copper lines leak under concrete slab foundations, we abandon the leaking underground pipes and run clean Uponor PEX-A overhead, completely avoiding jackhammer floor demolition.

  • Specialization: Overhead concrete slab leak bypasses
  • Applications: Kitchen/bath zones & basement buildouts
  • Benefit: Preserves finished tile, stone, and hardwood flooring

Frequently Asked Questions — Plumbing Services

What plumbing services does Just Plumbing, Inc. specialize in?

Just Plumbing specializes in infrastructure plumbing: whole-home repiping (replacing polybutylene, galvanized steel, Kitec, and corroded copper), overhead concrete slab leak bypasses, commercial and residential water heater installations, basement sump pump systems, and architectural kitchen/bath remodels.

How long does a whole-home repiping service take?

Most residential repipes are completed in just 1 to 2 business days. Our Master Plumber-led crew works in organized phases to ensure your domestic water service is fully restored at the end of each working day.

Does Just Plumbing handle municipal permits and inspections?

Yes. As a licensed Colorado Plumbing Contractor (#PC.0003531) led by Master Plumber Bill Clark (#MP.00600120), we manage all city and county permit applications, code compliance filings, and final municipal inspector sign-offs across all 28 Front Range service areas.
