Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Remodels

When should a plumber inspect a remodel site?

Consult a Master Plumber before finalizing cabinet or tile plans. Assessing floor joist directions, main stack locations, and existing pipe materials early prevents unexpected structural rework or costly layout changes.

Can I upgrade older plumbing lines during a renovation?

Remodeling is the ideal time to replace old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes with modern PEX Type A or copper. Since walls are already open, upgrading via partial repiping or a whole-home repipe adds minimal labor costs while preventing future leaks behind new tile.

Who manages the plumbing permits for my remodel?

Just Plumbing, Inc. operates as a registered Plumbing Contractor (#PC.0003531) in Colorado. We pull all necessary municipal plumbing permits and schedule required city rough-in and final inspections. Contact us today to discuss your renovation plans.
