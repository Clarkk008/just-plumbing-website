Kitchen & Bathroom Remodel Plumbing in Denver

★★★★☆ 4.7 stars based on 71 google reviews

Master Plumber Bill Clark delivers precise rough-in and finish plumbing for custom bathroom renovations, chef's kitchens, basement wet bars, and luxury home additions.

Architectural Precision for High-End Remodels

A luxury remodel is only as good as the infrastructure concealed behind the walls and underneath the subfloor. When you invest tens of thousands of dollars in designer Italian tile, custom quartz countertops, and high-end cabinetry, relying on a general handyman or tile installer to execute complex plumbing rough-ins is an enormous risk.

At Just Plumbing, Inc., Master Plumber Bill Clark personally oversees every rough-in and trim phase. We partner directly with homeowners, interior designers, and general contractors across Denver, Cherry Hills Village, Greenwood Village, and Golden to engineer flawless mechanical layouts that meet or exceed the International Plumbing Code (IPC) and local municipal requirements.

Our Specialized Remodeling Services

1. Luxury Master Bathroom Suites

We plumb multi-function luxury showers featuring pressure-balanced thermostatic mixing valves, ceiling-mounted rain heads, hand wands, and body sprays. We size supply lines to 3/4-inch to ensure unrestricted water volume, rough in freestanding soaking tub filler faucets, and install zero-threshold curbless linear drains with laser-leveled slope accuracy.

2. Chef's Kitchens & Island Expansions

Modernizing a kitchen often requires relocating the main sink to a center island, adding stove pot fillers, or installing commercial-grade gas ranges. We engineer code-compliant island loop venting to ensure whisper-quiet, rapid drainage without gurgling, and route dedicated water lines for reverse osmosis filtration, steam ovens, and secondary prep sinks.

3. Basement Bathroom & Wet Bar Additions

Finishing an open basement creates valuable living space but demands precision sub-slab drainage. We saw-cut concrete slabs to route 3-inch and 4-inch waste lines, connect to existing municipal sewer mains, install sewage ejector pumps where grades dictate, and plumb custom entertainment wet bars with bar sinks, ice makers, and beverage center drainage.

4. Complete Drain-Waste-Vent (DWV) Reconfiguration

Older homes in historic Denver neighborhoods often feature brittle cast-iron stacks and outdated galvanized vent pipes. When moving walls, we remove decaying metal and rebuild your DWV stack using smooth-walled PVC or ABS, eliminating chronic drain odors and guaranteeing code-approved air intake.

Pro Tip: Replace Old Pipes While Walls Are Open

The single best time to replace failing galvanized steel, polybutylene, or thin-walled copper is during the demolition phase of a remodel.

With wall studs and floor joists completely exposed, our Master Plumber team can perform a targeted partial repipe or a whole-home repipe using scale-proof Uponor PEX-A in a fraction of the time. You protect your brand-new tile and cabinetry from ever having to be torn apart for a future pipe leak.

Hot Water Demand Sizing for Remodels

Upgrading a bathroom with a 75-gallon soaking tub or a multi-head body-spray shower will rapidly overwhelm a standard 40-gallon atmospheric water heater. We evaluate your total fixture flow rate (gallons per minute) and size a continuous high-efficiency tankless water heater system to ensure endless hot water, even when multiple showers and appliances run simultaneously.

Frequently Asked Questions — Remodeling Plumbing

Why should a licensed Master Plumber handle remodel rough-ins instead of a general handyman?

Moving walls and reconfiguring sinks, showers, or toilets alters the slope, venting, and hydraulic balance of your home's Drain-Waste-Vent (DWV) system. Unlicensed handymen frequently install improper unvented S-traps or undersized supply lines that fail municipal building inspections and cause sewer gas leaks behind finished tile.

Should I replace old supply pipes during a kitchen or bathroom remodel?

Yes. When walls, subfloors, and ceilings are stripped down to the studs during demolition, replacing legacy galvanized steel, polybutylene, or aging copper with Uponor PEX-A is significantly faster and more economical than repiping after expensive tile and cabinetry have been installed.

Can you relocate an island sink without tearing up the entire ceiling below?

Yes. Island sinks require engineered venting because there is no vertical wall above the sink for a standard vent stack. We engineer code-compliant island loop vents (combination waste and vent) or install approved air admittance valves (AAVs) to ensure fast drainage without unnecessary ceiling demolition.

Does Just Plumbing pull permits for remodeling plumbing in Colorado?

Yes. All remodel rough-in and finish plumbing completed by Just Plumbing is fully permitted and scheduled for municipal rough and final inspections under Colorado Plumbing Contractor Registration #PC.0003531.
