Upgrading Your Home’s Plumbing Safely

If your home was built decades ago, the pipes running behind your walls are likely degrading from the inside out. At Just Plumbing, Inc., we map your home’s layout to install brand new, highly durable piping with surgical precision, dramatically improving your water pressure and protecting your property.

Signs You Need A Repipe:

  • Your home has gray/plastic Polybutylene pipes
  • Frequent pinhole leaks or sudden bursts
  • Rust-colored or foul-smelling water
  • Severe drop in water pressure when flushing/showering
  • Unexplained water damage on walls/ceilings

Our Targeted Repiping Solutions

Whether you are dealing with outdated materials or want to upgrade to premium plumbing, we have the expertise to handle it.

Polybutylene Pipe Replacement

Gray polybutylene pipes installed between 1978-1995 are well beyond their useful life. Many Colorado insurance companies are now dropping coverage for homes with these pipes. We fully extract and replace these dangerous lines.

Galvanized Pipe Replacement

Older galvanized steel pipes corrode from the inside out, restricting water flow and releasing rust into your drinking water. We upgrade these systems to restore your home's water pressure and purity.

PEX-A Repiping

We install premium, flexible PEX-A tubing. It is highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and corrosion, offering a 25+ year lifespan. This is the most cost-effective and minimally invasive option for Denver homes.

Type L Copper Repiping

For homeowners who prefer traditional metal, we install USA-made Type L copper. Copper offers natural antimicrobial properties and a proven lifespan of 50+ years for the ultimate peace of mind.

Repipe Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my house needs a full repipe?

If your home was built between the 1970s and 1990s with polybutylene pipes, or earlier with galvanized steel, you are at high risk for catastrophic leaks. Signs include constant low water pressure, rusty or discolored water, frequent pinhole leaks, and water damage on your ceilings or walls.

Will a repipe completely destroy my drywall?

No. A professional repipe requires surgical cuts, not demolition. We map out the exact route of your plumbing and make small, strategic incisions in the drywall. Furthermore, we actually hire and schedule a professional drywall patcher to restore your walls to base quality when the plumbing is done.

How long does a whole house repipe take?

Most standard residential repipes take between 1 to 3 days depending on the size of the home and the number of bathrooms. We ensure you have running water at the end of each work day so you can stay in your home during the process without being displaced.
