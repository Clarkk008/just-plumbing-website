Scalable Plumbing Subcontracting

Large-scale developments and custom architectural builds demand a plumbing subcontractor capable of taking pre-approved blueprints and executing the physical pipe routing with absolute precision. At Just Plumbing, Inc., we specialize in reading complex architectural drawings and translating them into efficient on-site production schedules—ensuring undergrounds, top-outs, and trim finishes are delivered seamlessly for single-family estates, tract communities, and multi-family projects across Greater Denver, Arvada, Lakewood, and Littleton.

Led by Master Plumber Bill Clark (License #MP.00600120) with over 30 years of field experience, we bring the supervisory logistics, commercial equipment capabilities, and structural expertise needed to build out pre-engineered prints without causing jobsite delays or costly municipal revision failures.

Our B2B Subcontractor Guarantee:

  • Blueprint Accuracy: Work is executed exactly to your architectural specs and Colorado IPC standards.
  • Zero-Delay Inspections: Rigorous hydrostatic and air-pressure testing performed prior to municipal inspections.
  • Fully Protected: Complete commercial general liability, auto, and workers' compensation insurance.
  • Transparent Bidding: Itemized, no-surprise proposals covering labor, materials, and fixture specs.

Multi-Family Developments & Condominiums

Multi-unit architectural footprints introduce complex engineering demands—including continuous recirculation loops, acoustic soundproofing isolation, individual submetering, and high-volume sanitary risers. Our team is equipped to handle complex multi-tenant builds:

  • Apartment Complexes & Multi-Family: Executing heavy-duty sanitary stacks, commercial gas manifold feeds, and high-output continuous hot water boiler systems through our multifamily services.
  • Luxury Condos & Townhomes: Routing clean, space-saving Uponor PEX-A distribution manifolds (backed by a 25-year material warranty and 5-year workmanship warranty) and rigid Type L copper piping directly to print specs. Explore our multi-unit work in the HOA & condo division and our Golden HOA Case Study.
  • Tract Home Communities: Providing rapid, repeatable rough-in production scheduling to keep large subdivisions moving through city inspection gates.

Our Phased Building Capabilities

From initial ground excavation to post-drywall fixture testing, we coordinate directly with your site managers across all three construction phases:

  • Phase 1: Underground & Slab Rough-In: Excavating and pitching main sewer trunks, foundation branch lines, sump pump basins, and sleeve penetrations prior to concrete pouring.
  • Phase 2: Top-Out & Framing Rough-In: Running main stack vents, water distribution manifolds, gas lines, and drain branches within raw wood framing before drywall.
  • Phase 3: Trim-Out & Finish Phase: Precision installation of high-end kitchen fixtures, commercial-grade pumps, tankless water heaters, and bath hardware prior to final code sign-off.

Frequently Asked Questions About New Construction Plumbing

What qualifications does Just Plumbing, Inc. hold for commercial developments?

Just Plumbing, Inc. operates under Colorado Plumbing Contractor Registration #PC.0003531 and Master Plumber License #MP.00600120. We carry the comprehensive commercial general liability, auto, and workers' compensation insurance required for large jobsite access.

How do you handle city rough-in and underground inspections?

We conduct rigorous hydrostatic and air-pressure testing on all DWV and water supply lines before scheduling municipal building inspectors, guaranteeing zero-delay pass rates across Denver Metro building departments.

Can Just Plumbing, Inc. review blueprints and submit competitive bids?
