Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heaters

Should I repair or replace my water heater?

If your unit is under 8 to 10 years old and has a repairable issue (such as a faulty heating element, thermocouple, or thermostat), repairing is cost-effective. However, if the main tank is leaking, heavily rusted, or over 10 years old, replacing it is required to prevent catastrophic basement flooding.

Are tankless water heaters suitable for Colorado's cold winters?

Yes, high-efficiency tankless water heaters are engineered to handle Colorado's cold incoming groundwater temperature. Proper sizing by a licensed Master Plumber ensures endless hot water even during severe winter freezes.

How does hard water affect water heaters in the Denver Metro area?

Hard mineral water along Colorado's Front Range causes heavy sediment accumulation inside water heater tanks. Annual flushing or tankless descaling prevents heating element burnout, popping noises, and premature tank failure. For more detailed answers on maintaining your system, check out our full FAQ page.
