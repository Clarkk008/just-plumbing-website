Scalable New Construction Plumbing Subcontracting

Large-scale developments and custom architectural builds demand a plumbing subcontractor capable of taking pre-approved blueprints and executing the physical pipe routing with absolute precision. At Just Plumbing, Inc., we specialize in reading complex architectural drawings and translating them into efficient on-site production schedules—ensuring undergrounds, top-outs, and trim finishes are delivered seamlessly for single-family estates, tract communities, and multi-family projects across Greater Denver, Arvada, Lakewood, and Littleton.

Led by Master Plumber Bill Clark (License #MP.00600120) with over 30 years of field experience, we bring the supervisory logistics, commercial equipment capabilities, and structural expertise needed to build out pre-engineered prints without causing jobsite delays or costly revisions.

Multi-Family Developments, Apartments & Condominiums

Multi-unit architectural footprints introduce complex engineering demands—including continuous recirculation loops, acoustic soundproofing isolation, individual submetering, and high-volume sanitary risers. Our fully licensed, bonded, and insured team is engineered to handle complex multi-tenant builds:

  • Apartment Complexes & Multi-Family Structures: Executing heavy-duty sanitary stacks, commercial gas manifold feeds, and high-output continuous hot water boilers built for intensive multi-tenant load factors.
  • Luxury Condos & Townhomes: Routing clean, space-saving PEX Type A manifold distributions, acoustic-dampened waste drops, and precision overhead fire-suppression interface plumbing directly to print specs.
  • Tract Home Communities: Providing rapid, repeatable rough-in production scheduling to keep large community subdivisions moving seamlessly through city inspection gates.

Our Phased Building Capabilities

From initial ground excavation to post-drywall fixture testing, we coordinate directly with your project managers across all three construction phases:

  • Phase 1: Underground & Slab Rough-In: Excavating and pitching main sewer trunks, foundation branch lines, and sleeve penetrations prior to concrete pouring.
  • Phase 2: Top-Out & Framing Rough-In: Running main stack vents, water distribution manifolds, gas lines, and drain branches within raw wood framing before drywall installation.
  • Phase 3: Trim-Out & Finish Phase: Precision installation of high-end kitchen fixtures, commercial-grade pumps, tankless water heaters, and bath hardware prior to final code sign-off.

Frequently Asked Questions About New Construction Plumbing

What qualifications does Just Plumbing, Inc. hold for commercial and residential developments?

Just Plumbing, Inc. operates under Colorado Plumbing Contractor Registration #PC.0003531 and Master Plumber License #MP.00600120. We carry comprehensive commercial general liability, auto, and workers' compensation insurance required for large jobsite access.

How do you handle city rough-in and underground inspections?

We conduct rigorous hydrostatic and air-pressure testing on all DWV and water supply lines before scheduling municipal building inspectors, guaranteeing zero-delay pass rates across Denver Metro building departments.

Can Just Plumbing, Inc. review blueprints and submit competitive bids?

Yes. General contractors and developers can submit digital CAD/PDF blueprints directly to our estimating team for itemized, transparent bid proposals covering material takeoffs, labor schedules, and fixture specs.
