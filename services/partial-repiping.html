Targeted Plumbing Upgrades & Repairs

If you call a massive national repiping corporation with a localized leak or an unfinished basement you want to update, they will likely turn you down. Their corporate overhead is too high to accept targeted, partial repiping projects.

While a complete whole-home repiping overhaul is ideal for older structures, your budget or timeline may call for a more focused solution. At Just Plumbing, Inc., we specialize in partial home repiping—replacing critical, high-risk, or failing sections of your plumbing network to keep your property functional and dry without the expense of a full system replacement.

Whether you are dealing with brittle polybutylene sections in a crawlspace, corroded galvanized lines in an unfinished basement, or updating pipe routes during a kitchen or bath renovation, Master Plumber Bill Clark provides precise, code-compliant integrations using flexible PEX Type A and rigid Copper materials.

When is a Partial Repipe the Best Choice?

A partial repipe offers a highly strategic alternative under several common household scenarios:

  • Exposed Pipeline Access: Updating fully accessible lines in your basement, utility room, or crawlspace before micro-fractures or pinhole leaks develop.
  • Preparation for Remodeling: Upgrading buried supply lines behind shower surrounds or kitchen cabinetry before tile and drywall are permanently sealed during renovations.
  • Localized Damage Control: Swapping out single runs of problematic polybutylene or localized corrosion points that trigger recurring leaks.
  • Pressure Optimization: Replacing old galvanized main manifold routes to restore pure water flow to key fixtures or prior to installing new water heater systems.

Frequently Asked Questions About Partial Repiping

Can you connect new PEX pipes to existing copper or galvanized lines?

Yes. Using approved transition fittings (such as dielectric unions or copper-to-PEX adapters), we safely join modern PEX Type A expansion piping to existing sound copper or steel lines without causing galvanic corrosion.

Is a partial repipe enough to fix low water pressure?

If low water pressure is caused by heavy mineral rust clogging a specific main line or exposed basement manifold, replacing that key section will significantly increase flow to your fixtures. If the entire system is corroded, we may evaluate options for a whole-home repipe.

Does a partial repipe require shutting off the water for multiple days?

No. Partial repiping projects are typically completed in 1 to 2 days, and Just Plumbing, Inc. ensures water service is restored by the end of each workday.
