Signs Your Galvanized Pipes Have Reached End-of-Life

1. Severe Drops in Water Pressure

As zinc coatings degrade, internal rust accumulates into thick mineral nodules (tuberculation). Over decades, a 3/4-inch supply line can constrict down to the width of a drinking straw.

2. Brown, Rusty, or Metallic-Tasting Water

Discolored water after being away from home, rust rings in toilets, or stained laundry are direct indicators of active iron shedding inside your domestic water lines.

3. Corrosion Around Threaded Joints

Galvanized lines fail first at the threaded connections where metal was cut during installation. Look for white crusting, calcium buildup, or rust streaks on exposed basement pipes.
