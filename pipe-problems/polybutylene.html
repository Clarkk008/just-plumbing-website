Understanding Polybutylene (Quest) Pipe Failures

How to Identify Polybutylene in Your Home

Polybutylene is typically a flexible, dull gray plastic pipe (rarely silver or matte blue). Look for the stamp "PB2110", "Quest", or "Qest" near your water heater connections, basement ceiling joists, or main water shutoff valve.

The Internal Breakdown Mechanism

Unlike copper or PEX, polybutylene reacts chemically with municipal water disinfectants (such as chlorine and chloramines). This reaction causes micro-fractures inside the pipe wall and brittle acetal fittings that give way suddenly under normal pressure.

Real Estate & Home Insurance Impact

Disclosing polybutylene plumbing during a home sale often leads to buyer price drops or failed inspections. Most Colorado insurers now require full polybutylene replacement before issuing a clean homeowner's policy.
