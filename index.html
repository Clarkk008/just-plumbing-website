Common Pipe Problems in Colorado Homes

Failing pipes cause quiet water damage, lower resale value, and trigger insurance cancellations. Learn how to identify your system and how we fix it permanently:

Polybutylene (Quest) Pipes

Installed 1978–1995. Chlorine degrades brittle gray plastic pipes from the inside out, causing sudden bursts and insurance denials across Highlands Ranch & Parker. See our Highlands Ranch Case Study.

Polybutylene Guide »

Galvanized Steel Pipes

Found in pre-1960s Denver homes. Internal rust chokes off water pressure, discolors water, and rots out threaded joints in historic neighborhoods. Read our Wash Park Case Study.

Galvanized Guide »

Kitec Composite Plumbing

Installed 1995–2007. Yellow brass fittings undergo dezincification, creating white meringue deposits and sudden fittings blowouts in Broomfield & Thornton. Check out our Thornton Case Study.

Kitec Guide »

Copper Pinhole Leaks

Aggressive water chemistry and Front Range water pressure pit thin copper lines, leading to recurring drywall patches and expensive under-slab leaks. Explore our Arvada Case Study.

Copper Leaks Guide »
