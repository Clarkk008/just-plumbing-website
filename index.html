Colorado Pipe Problems We Solve Permanently

Colorado's expansive bentonite soils, high water tables, and municipal chloramines aggressively degrade legacy piping materials. Learn how to identify your system:

Polybutylene (Quest) Pipes

Installed 1978–1995. Chlorine degrades brittle gray plastic pipes from the inside out, causing sudden blowouts and insurance cancellations. See our Highlands Ranch Case Study.

Polybutylene Diagnosis »

Galvanized Steel Pipes

Found in pre-1965 Denver homes. Internal rust chokes off water pressure, discolors drinking water, and rots out threaded joints. Read our real-world transformation in the Park Hill Case Study.

Galvanized Steel Guide »

Kitec Composite Plumbing

Installed 1995–2007. Recalled orange/blue composite lines with yellow brass fittings that undergo dezincification, threatening real estate sales. Review our Thornton Case Study.

Kitec Identification »

Copper Pinhole Leaks

Chloramines and high municipal water pressure pit thin copper walls, leading to expensive drywall damage and sub-slab leaks. Explore our non-invasive Arvada Slab Leak Case Study.

Copper Leak Solutions »

Frequently Asked Questions About Just Plumbing, Inc.

Who owns and operates Just Plumbing, Inc.?

Just Plumbing, Inc. is a family-owned, Colorado-operated contractor led by Master Plumber Bill Clark and Operations Director Shelly Clark. Bill brings over 30 years of hands-on piping expertise to every single repiping project under Colorado Master Plumber License #MP.00600120 and Contractor Registration #PC.0003531. Learn more on our About Us page.

How long does a typical whole-home repipe take?

Most standard single-family residential repipes take between 1 and 2 business days. We isolate plumbing in organized daily phases so your domestic water service is safely restored by 5:00 PM every evening, allowing you to stay comfortably in your home throughout the process without being displaced.

Will a repipe ruin my walls and ceilings?

No. We make clean, incisions (typically 6"x6" to 12"x12") only where necessary to access pipe chases. Unlike other plumbing companies that leave you with open walls, we hire and schedule a professional drywall finisher to patch, mud, and apply matching texture to your walls as part of our turnkey service.
