Neighborhood Case Study • Arvada, CO

Arvada Copper Pinhole Leak Reroute

Project execution and write-up by Master Plumber Bill Clark (MP.00600120)

Abandoning pitted, leaking under-slab copper lines and executing a surgical overhead PEX-A repipe to save the home's foundation and finished flooring.

The Problem: Pitted Copper & Slab Leaks

The homeowner of this 1970s split-level home in Arvada noticed a sudden spike in their monthly water bill accompanied by a persistently warm spot beneath their lower-level laminate flooring.

An on-site inspection by Master Plumber Bill Clark confirmed the diagnosis: a hot water slab leak. The home's original Type M copper piping had developed severe internal pitting corrosion, causing recurring copper pinhole leaks under the concrete slab. Highly oxygenated Front Range groundwater and mineral friction had eroded the pipe walls from the inside out. Spot-repairing the line would require jackhammering finished floors, only for another pinhole to erupt nearby months later.

The Master Plumber Solution

Instead of jackhammering the concrete foundation to chase isolated leaks, Just Plumbing executed an overhead reroute through our whole-home repiping process. We permanently capped the failing copper lines beneath the slab and installed a brand new Uponor PEX-A expansion system (backed by a 25-year material warranty and 5-year workmanship warranty) routed safely overhead through interior walls and ceiling cavities. Where exposed metallic transitions were required by local code near the water heater system, we integrated heavy-duty Type L rigid copper.

  • Under-Slab Abandonment: We safely isolated, capped, and drained the compromised under-slab copper supply lines, permanently neutralizing the leak without structural demolition.
  • Overhead PEX-A Reroute: We fished highly flexible, full-port Uponor PEX-A tubing through attic spaces and framing cavities, dropping dedicated lines down to the kitchen and bathrooms.
  • Scale & Pitting Immunity: PEX-A expansion piping is completely inert to aggressive mineral chemistry and velocity friction, eliminating future pitting leaks. Learn more in our PEX-A vs. PEX-B Guide.
  • Turnkey Drywall Restoration: Our scheduled drywall patching team repaired every surgical incision back to smooth, paint-ready condition.

Project Specifications Summary

Location: Arvada, CO (Jefferson County)
Property Type: 1970s Split-Level (Concrete Slab on Grade)
Original Piping: Thin-Wall Type M Copper (Under-Slab Pitting)
New Material Installed: Uponor PEX-A Full-Port Overhead System
Timeline: Completed in 2 Days (Water Restored Nightly)
Final Result: Slab preserved; leak permanently eliminated
