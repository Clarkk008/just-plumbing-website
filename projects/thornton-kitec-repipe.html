Thornton Kitec Plumbing System Replacement

Drywall Restoration Full Professional Patching & Texture Included Outcome Master Plumber Certificate Issued to Title Co.

The Challenge: The Home Inspection Roadblock

Selling a home built between 1995 and 2007 along the Front Range comes with a specific anxiety for real estate agents: Kitec plumbing. In this Thornton subdivision home, a buyer’s inspector immediately spotted the distinctive bright orange and blue tubing near the basement water heater. Furthermore, the yellow brass crimp rings on the supply manifold were already exhibiting severe dezincification—a chemical reaction leaving behind a white, powdery crust that signals imminent catastrophic blowout.

Because Kitec was subject to a massive nationwide recall, the buyer’s mortgage lender refused to underwrite the loan, and the insurance carrier refused to issue a policy unless the system was completely replaced. With closing scheduled in just two weeks, the sellers called Master Plumber Bill Clark at Just Plumbing to save the deal.

The Master Plumber Diagnosis

Upon inspection, Bill confirmed that the proprietary Kitec fittings were failing globally. Because Kitec was manufactured to non-standard dimensional sizes, it cannot be safely patched or transitioned with standard PEX crimps. Repairing the single manifold was impossible; the entire distribution network needed extraction.

Bill immediately scheduled an expedited whole-home repipe using premium Uponor PEX-A expansion tubing and coordinated directly with the City of Thornton building department to fast-track the necessary plumbing permit.

The 1.5-Day Expedited Execution

Day 1: Full System Bypass & Extraction

Operating with urgency to protect the closing timeline, our crew arrived at 7:00 AM, deployed floor protection, and initiated wall cuts. We entirely abandoned the old Kitec manifold in the basement and ran fresh 3/4-inch Uponor PEX-A hot and cold trunk lines. By routing flexible lines vertically through interior partition walls, we successfully fed the upstairs bathrooms with minimal drywall intrusion. Domestic water was restored by late afternoon.

Day 2: Trim-Out, Inspection & Certification

We spent the morning connecting all remaining fixture branches (toilets, vanities, washing machine, and exterior hose bibs) utilizing full-port ProPEX expansion rings for maximum water volume. By 1:00 PM, Bill Clark completed the 100 PSI hydrostatic pressure test and walked the property with the City of Thornton inspector, securing an instant "Pass" on the permit. Before leaving, Bill drafted the official Master Plumber Repiping Certificate and sent it directly to the closing title company.

Real Estate Closing Rescued

A pending home sale is stressful enough without the threat of a $10,000+ plumbing disaster derailing the mortgage underwriting.

Because Just Plumbing is owned and operated directly by a Master Plumber, we bypassed the bloated scheduling delays common with national franchises. Our turnkey solution—including the rapid permit pull, the 1.5-day installation, and the scheduling of our professional drywall patcher—allowed the sellers to close their transaction on time with zero outstanding contingencies.

The Outcome

The buyers moved into a home with a brand-new, code-compliant Uponor PEX-A plumbing system backed by a 25-year manufacturer warranty and our 5-year workmanship guarantee. The sellers avoided a collapsed sale and successfully transferred the title without friction.

Frequently Asked Questions — Thornton Kitec Repiping

Why did the home inspector flag the Kitec plumbing in this Thornton home?

Kitec composite piping was the subject of a massive nationwide class-action recall due to its high failure rate. Specifically, its yellow brass fittings undergo dezincification, creating a brittle crust that eventually causes the fittings to blow out entirely. Because it's a known defect, lenders and insurance companies often require its removal prior to closing.

Could the failing Kitec fittings just be patched or repaired?

No. Kitec pipe was manufactured to unique imperial-metric dimensions that do not match standard PEX fittings. Furthermore, because dezincification affects the entire system simultaneously, patching one fitting only shifts the hydraulic stress to the next weak joint. Whole-home repiping is the only code-compliant solution.

How quickly was Just Plumbing able to replace the system to save the real estate closing?

We completed the entire extraction and Uponor PEX-A installation in just 1.5 days. Master Plumber Bill Clark immediately provided the required completion certificate and passed the City of Thornton plumbing inspection, allowing the title company to proceed with the sale on schedule.
