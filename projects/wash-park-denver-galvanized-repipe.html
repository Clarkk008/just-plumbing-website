Neighborhood Case Study • Denver, CO

1928 Wash Park Bungalow Galvanized Repipe

Project execution and write-up by Master Plumber Bill Clark (MP.00600120)

Preserving historic plaster while eliminating nearly 100 years of internal pipe corrosion and low water pressure.

The Problem: "Straw-Sized" Water Flow & Rusty Water

This classic 1928 brick bungalow near Washington Park (Denver) was suffering from extreme water pressure loss. Whenever the homeowner ran the washing machine in the basement, the second-story bathroom shower would drop to a trickle. Furthermore, water sat overnight in the lines and flowed rust-colored for the first 30 seconds every morning.

An on-site inspection by Master Plumber Bill Clark identified original galvanized steel plumbing. Decades of mineral deposits and internal rust had choked the 3/4-inch supply lines down to an internal opening of less than 1/4 inch.

The Master Plumber Solution

Demolishing original lath-and-plaster walls was not an option. Through our specialized whole-home repiping process, Just Plumbing executed a surgical, minimal-penetration repipe utilizing commercial-grade Uponor PEX-A expansion tubing (backed by a 25-year material warranty and our 5-year workmanship warranty) along with rigid Type L Copper at the water heater transitions:

  • Strategic Access Cuts: Made small, precision square cuts only where vertical risers connected to fixture branches.
  • Flexible Line Fishing: Fed flexible PEX-A through existing wall cavities from the basement upward without disturbing historic plaster crown moulding. Learn why full-port expansion systems outperform crimp fittings in our PEX-A vs. PEX-B Guide.
  • Dedicated Home-Run Manifolds: Installed a central manifold in the basement utility room, ensuring balanced, high-pressure flow to all 2.5 bathrooms simultaneously.
  • Turnkey Drywall Repair: Professional patchers repaired and textured all access points, leaving the home ready for finish paint.

Project Specifications Summary

Neighborhood: Washington Park, Denver, CO
Property Type: 1928 Brick Bungalow (2,100 sq. ft., 2.5 Bath)
Original Piping: Corroded Galvanized Steel
New Material Installed: Uponor ProPEX Type A Expansion Tubing
Timeline: 2 Business Days (Water restored nightly)
Final Result: Water pressure doubled; zero rust; 25-year material warranty
