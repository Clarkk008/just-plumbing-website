Neighborhood Case Study • Thornton, CO

Thornton Kitec Plumbing Extraction

Project execution and write-up by Master Plumber Bill Clark (MP.00600120)

Removing the recalled orange and blue composite piping to prevent catastrophic brass fitting failure, restore water pressure, and clear a real estate transaction.

The Problem: Dezincification & Recalled Fittings

This spacious late-90s build in Thornton, CO was facing a major hurdle. The homeowner was preparing to list the property, but a pre-inspection revealed the entire house was plumbed with Kitec plumbing—the notorious bright orange and blue composite piping subject to a massive North American class-action recall.

The issue wasn't just the composite tubing; it was the high-zinc brass fittings. Kitec brass fittings undergo a chemical breakdown called dezincification when exposed to water, causing zinc to leach out of the metal alloy. This leaves the fitting brittle and coated with a white, powdery meringue corrosion, inevitably leading to sudden fitting blowouts behind finished drywall. Realizing the home was uninsurable and unsellable in its current state, the homeowner called Just Plumbing.

The Master Plumber Solution

To clear the home for a seamless real estate closing, Master Plumber Bill Clark and our team performed a code-compliant whole-home repipe using Uponor PEX-A expansion tubing (backed by a 25-year manufacturer material warranty and our 5-year workmanship warranty).

  • Surgical Mitigation: We carefully traced the Kitec lines through joist spaces and made strategic, small square cuts in the drywall to extract the defective fittings and pull new full-port PEX-A tubing.
  • Modern Manifold Install: We replaced the outdated Kitec distribution block in the utility room with a high-flow Uponor PEX-A manifold, balancing water volume across all three bathrooms. Learn why full-flow expansion systems outperform crimp fittings in our PEX-A vs. PEX-B Guide.
  • Mechanical Transitions: At the water heater system, we integrated code-compliant Type L Copper stubs.
  • Inspection Clearance: We coordinated directly with City of Thornton building inspectors to sign off on the permit quickly so the listing timeline was not delayed.
  • Turnkey Drywall Repair: Our dedicated patching crew sealed, taped, and textured every access incision, ensuring the home was left in immaculate condition for prospective buyers.

Project Specifications Summary

Neighborhood: Thornton, CO (Adams County)
Property Type: Late 1990s Multi-Level Home (2,600 sq. ft., 3 Bath)
Original Piping: Kitec Composite (Orange/Blue) & Dezincifying Brass Fittings
New Material Installed: Uponor ProPEX Type A Expansion Tubing
Timeline: 2.5 Days (Water Restored Nightly)
Final Result: Passed city inspection; property cleared for sale
