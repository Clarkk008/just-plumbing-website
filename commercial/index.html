Frequently Asked Questions — Commercial Repiping

Do commercial tenants have to relocate during a building repipe?

No. Just Plumbing executes multi-story repipes in vertical 'stacks'. By isolating specific units vertically, we complete drywall removal and rough-ins during standard business hours and restore domestic water service every evening, preventing costly hotel relocations.

Does Just Plumbing handle municipal permits for large commercial projects?

Yes. Master Plumber Bill Clark directly files all necessary mechanical and plumbing permits, coordinates phased inspections with city officials, and ensures all structural fire-stopping code requirements are met before drywall is restored.

Can you repipe a building that has a mix of commercial and residential units?

Yes. We frequently engineer hybrid repiping solutions. We install commercial-grade Type L Copper for the main building risers and ground-floor retail spaces, and transition to flexible Uponor PEX-A inside the individual residential units to minimize drywall demolition.
