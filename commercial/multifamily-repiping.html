The Turnkey Multifamily Repipe Process

1. Engineering Assessment & Plan Submissions

We map all central supply risers, branch lines, and shutoff valves. We pull all municipal building permits and structure a phased construction schedule customized to your property layout.

2. Tenant Coordination & Dust Protection

We provide customized notification templates for residents. Our crews mask off work zones, cover tenant furnishings with heavy plastic, and vacuum work areas at the end of every shift.

3. Upgrading to Uponor PEX-A Systems

Failing lines are replaced with expansion-fit Uponor PEX-A lines, eliminating pinhole leaks, improving hot water delivery speed, and restoring full water pressure across upper-floor units.
