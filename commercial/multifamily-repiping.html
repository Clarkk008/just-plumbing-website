Apartment & Multifamily Repiping Services

Replace defective plumbing across entire apartment communities with zero tenant displacement and water restored daily.

Why Property Managers Choose Just Plumbing

100% In-House Master Plumber

Every stack is replaced and inspected under Master Plumber Bill Clark (#MP.00600120), guaranteeing code compliance and rapid permit closures.

Phased Stack Sequencing

We replace vertical plumbing risers in organized vertical stacks to minimize unit entries and preserve uninterrupted daily water access.

Insurance Premium Reduction

Eliminating failing polybutylene and Kitec removes high-risk insurance exclusions, safeguarding property value and reducing liability.

The Turnkey Multifamily Repipe Process

1. Engineering Assessment & Plan Submissions

We map all central supply risers, branch lines, and shutoff valves. We pull all municipal building permits and structure a phased construction schedule customized to your property layout, integrating high-capacity water heater and boiler systems.

2. Tenant Coordination & Dust Protection

We provide customized notification templates for residents. Our crews mask off work zones, cover tenant furnishings with heavy plastic, and vacuum work areas at the end of every shift.

3. Upgrading to Uponor PEX-A Systems

Failing lines are replaced with expansion-fit Uponor PEX-A lines (backed by a 25-year manufacturer material warranty and 5-year workmanship warranty) and rigid Type L Copper in central mechanical spaces, eliminating recurring pinhole leaks, improving hot water delivery speed, and restoring full water pressure across upper-floor units.

4. Proven Multi-Tenant Case Studies

Explore how we eliminate defective polybutylene, corroded galvanized risers, and Kitec plumbing in multi-unit properties by reviewing our Golden HOA Condo Case Study and complementary HOA & condominium solutions.
