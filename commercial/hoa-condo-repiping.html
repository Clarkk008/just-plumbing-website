Protecting Your HOA's Master Insurance Policy

For Homeowner Association (HOA) Boards and Property Management companies, a multi-unit plumbing failure is a financial nightmare. In condominium structures, a single pinhole leak in a third-floor central riser can cascade downward, causing tens of thousands of dollars in drywall, flooring, and mold remediation damage to the units below.

When a building experiences multiple water damage claims, commercial insurance carriers will drastically raise the community's master policy premiums—or worse, drop coverage entirely. At Just Plumbing, Inc., Master Plumber Bill Clark partners with HOA Boards to permanently eliminate copper riser leaks and recalled polybutylene, securing your community's insurability and property values.

Clear Board Communication & Reserve Planning

We understand that securing funding for a capital improvement project requires transparency and consensus among board members and residents. Unlike franchise plumbing companies that send commissioned sales reps, Master Plumber Bill Clark personally attends your HOA Board meetings.

  • We present clear, engineering-based diagnostic audits of your building's current piping condition.
  • We provide exact, fixed-price commercial bids to assist with reserve study planning.
  • Shelly Clark coordinates with property management to distribute phase calendars and 48-hour entry notices to all unit owners.

The "Vertical Stack" Phased Execution

Displacing an entire condominium building of residents to hotels is not a viable option. We engineer our commercial repipes using a vertical stack methodology.

1. Phased Isolation

We isolate the water supply to a single vertical column of units (e.g., Units 104, 204, and 304) at one time. The rest of the building remains completely operational. Water in the active work zone is shut off only during business hours (8:00 AM to 4:30 PM).

2. Hybrid Material Installation

We install heavy-wall Type L Copper for the main central building risers to handle high-velocity hot water recirculation and meet commercial fire codes. Inside the individual condo units, we branch off using highly flexible Uponor PEX-A tubing to route water to kitchens and baths with minimal drywall destruction.

3. Nightly Water Restoration

Every evening, our crews conduct pressure testing and restore full domestic water service to the affected units, allowing homeowners to return from work to a functional home.

Commercial Fire-Stopping & Turnkey Drywall

When pipes pass through shared floors and ceilings in a condo building, those penetrations must be sealed with commercial intumescent fire-caulk to prevent the spread of smoke and flame. Bill Clark ensures 100% compliance with municipal fire marshal codes.

Furthermore, Just Plumbing handles all drywall restoration. We hire and schedule professional commercial drywall finishers to repair, tape, and texture all access holes in the common hallways and inside the resident units—providing the HOA with a true turnkey solution.

Frequently Asked Questions — HOA Repiping

Will Just Plumbing present to our HOA Board of Directors?

Yes. Master Plumber Bill Clark personally attends HOA board meetings to present structural plumbing audits, explain the engineering behind the proposed riser replacement, and outline the exact financial and scheduling milestones for the project.

Do condo owners have to move out during the repiping process?

No. We utilize a phased 'vertical stack' approach. By working on a single column of stacked units simultaneously, we confine water shutoffs to regular business hours (8:00 AM – 4:30 PM) and restore full domestic water service every evening.

Who handles the drywall patching inside the individual condo units?

Just Plumbing provides a true turnkey solution. We hire and schedule professional commercial drywall finishers to repair, mud, and texture all access holes in common areas and inside individual resident units, eliminating the need for the HOA to coordinate secondary contractors.

How does an HOA repipe affect our master insurance policy?

Chronic pinhole leaks lead to massive water damage claims, causing insurance carriers to drastically increase premiums or drop master policies entirely. Completing a Master Plumber-certified repipe eliminates these liabilities, often resulting in policy renewals and lowered premium surcharges.
